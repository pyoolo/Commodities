# Macro-and-Cross-Asset

How commodities interact with the rest of the financial system.

## Scope
- **Inflation**: commodities as a hedge, pass-through to CPI/PPI, breakevens
- **US dollar**: the pricing-currency effect and the demand channel
- **Rates**: real rates and gold, cost of carry, financing of inventory
- **Business cycle**: copper and oil as leading indicators, China credit impulse
- **Equity links**: producers vs the underlying commodity, beta and operating leverage
- **Positioning & flows**: CFTC COT, index rebalancing (BCOM, S&P GSCI), ETF/ETC flows
- **Regimes**: correlation with equities and bonds conditional on the inflation regime

## Benchmarks
Bloomberg Commodity Index (BCOM) · S&P GSCI (energy-heavy) · Rogers International Commodity Index
Note how differently they weight energy — index choice drives most of the performance difference.
